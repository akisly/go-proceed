## Scope and mode

`apps/landing/app/page.tsx` is a Persuade surface for the permanent public
GoProceed landing.

## Audience, job and action

The primary visitor is an owner, commercial director or head of PTV at a
Ukrainian electrical/MEP subcontractor. They need to understand the evidence
and closure mechanism through one credible work example, then begin a bounded
pilot enquiry through `#pilot`.

## Proof and constraints

The proof is the route `R-041 → EV-0248 → DR-0091 → CL-017 → draft act`, shown
through a drawing, field image, external review and provenance receipt. Product
boundaries from `PRODUCT.md` remain explicit. No customer proof, pricing or
outcome metric is invented.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Evidence Journey inherits the Evidence Atlas world and turns it into one
continuous evidence rail. The memorable moment is the hand-off from the site
photograph into a no-account technical-supervision decision, visibly resolving
the stage from blocked to allowed.

## Unresolved decisions

the visitor must still send it.
