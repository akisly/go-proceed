# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are owners, commercial directors and heads of PTV at
Ukrainian specialist electrical and MEP subcontractors, typically coordinating
several active construction sites. Foremen capture evidence on site; internal
PTV staff prepare and check requirements; technical supervision reviews exact
evidence through a narrow external surface.

## Product Purpose

GoProceed helps specialist construction teams make performed work ready for
acceptance. It connects the requirement that applies to a work stage, the
evidence captured before concealment, the attributable review decision, the
recorded stage closure and the facts used to prepare a draft statutory act.

Success means the team can show why a stage was allowed or refused without
reconstructing the history from chat messages, drives and spreadsheets after
the construction has been covered.

## Positioning

GoProceed is an evidence and closure control, not another file store or generic
construction CRM. A blocking requirement belongs to a specific work stage and
must be satisfied before that stage can be recorded as closed; the evidence and
decision keep their author, time, place, source and relationship to the work.

## Operating Context

The product is evaluated and used around drawings, requirement sources such as
DBN references, work registers, hidden or covered stages, mobile site capture,
technical-supervision decisions and statutory act drafts. Current field capture
is online-only. External reviewers receive a protected link with the exact
scope needed to accept, return or request clarification without joining the
contractor's workspace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- v0.1 connects hand-entered work lines, loaded requirements, required mobile
  evidence, refused stage closure, draft acts and protected external decisions.
- v0.1 blocks recorded stage closure; it does not block physical work or
  payment-presentation eligibility.
- Current field capture requires an active network connection; an unuploaded
  original is not durable evidence.
- A draft act is not a signed document and GoProceed does not claim qualified
  electronic signature support.
- Internal review, immutable evidence packages, partial acceptance, claim
  segments and value-at-risk projection belong to v0.2 and must not be shown as
  current v0.1 capabilities.
- Demonstration records use plausible Ukrainian construction language and must
  be labelled whenever they could be mistaken for customer or production data.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is GoProceed. Public copy is in Ukrainian, direct and factual:
specific work, requirements and decisions instead of hype or broad software
claims. The landing-page craft bar is informed by Linear's product-led restraint
and Folio's large, legible product scenes, while its content and identity remain
specific to construction evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains canonical domain and delivery documentation, typed
contracts, sample Ukrainian construction records, an implemented online field
capture path and a no-account external evidence-review surface. It also contains
approved synthetic blueprint, site-evidence and receipt assets.

There are no approved customer logos, testimonials, outcome benchmarks, prices
or conversion statistics. Future marketing work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Show the exact requirement before asking a site worker for evidence.
- Keep evidence attributable and visibly connected to the work and decision.
- Refuse unsupported recorded closure without claiming to stop physical work.
- Give external reviewers the narrowest useful surface with no account burden.
- State version boundaries as plainly as product benefits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public and operational web surfaces must work on desktop and Android-class
mobile browsers, remain keyboard operable, expose visible focus, avoid
colour-only status and respect reduced-motion preferences. Ukrainian copy must
remain legible at 360px without horizontal scrolling.
