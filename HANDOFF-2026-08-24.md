## 3. The owner's standing instructions, including the ones I got wrong first

These are corrections, not preferences. Two of them were given twice because the
first time they were not followed.

1. **UI components come one-to-one from shadcn/ui** and the named reference
   repos — [`ln-dev7/circle`](https://github.com/ln-dev7/circle),
   [`satnaing/shadcn-admin`](https://github.com/satnaing/shadcn-admin). Do not
   hand-roll a substitute and do not extend the older hand-written kit because
   it is already there. The Shadcn UI MCP tools (`get_component`) are the
   sanctioned way to take one.
2. **Tables are TanStack Table.** Not hand-written `<table>` markup.
3. **Validation is zod**, and a form validates with the *same* schema the route
   parses — not a restatement of the rules.
4. **Latest versions, no pins.** This is why zod is 4.4.3 and TanStack is 9
   even though shadcn-admin pins TanStack 8.
5. **The hierarchy comes from [`makeplane/plane`](https://github.com/makeplane/plane)**
   — AGPL, so **structure only, never its code**. `app/**` is routes only;
   components are kebab-case under `src/components/<domain>/`; anything that
   talks to an API is a `src/services/*.service.ts`. From circle and
   shadcn-admin we take **only the implementation of pages and components**, not
   their `features/` layout.

## 4. What shipped, and the two defects that reached a user

**D0** — the `/dash` shell: rail, mobile drawer, workspace identity, profile,
and **the first way to sign out this product has ever had**.

**D1** — evidence read on both planes. `GET /v1/assignments/{id}/evidence`
(member, short-lived signed URLs) and `GET /external/evidence` (external, a
same-origin byte stream). The assignments register, the evidence screen, and the
control that issues the review link — which until then was reachable only by
curl. A browser audit walks the whole loop, ending with a second browser context
carrying no cookies opening the link and seeing the image.

**D2** — the money view at `/dash/projects/{projectId}`: what is blocked, why,
and for how long, per currency, never summed across currencies.

**#49** — the foundation above.

Two defects reached a real user before anything caught them, and both were found
by *running* the product rather than reading it:

- **The evidence card rendered `origin_not_distinguished`** — a raw identifier,
  and the only value a phone capture can produce — to a Ukrainian-speaking ПТВ,
  while its fidelity test stayed green, because the test read migration `0015`
  and `0043` had widened the constraint. Both fidelity tests now read
  `pg_constraint`. **Postgres does not store a CHECK in the spelling the
  migration writes**: `check (col in (…))` is normalised to `= ANY (ARRAY[…])`,
  and six migrations already write it that way by hand, so a text parser is
  blind to the next widening.
- **«Отримано сервером» rendered in the server's timezone** — correct on a Kyiv
  laptop, three hours early on Vercel, with no zone marker, in the one field the
  screen exists to make trustworthy. The six-viewport visual pass structurally
  could not catch it; rendering the component under `TZ=UTC` did.

### 5.1 The requirement library — decided: «робоча документація»

The library is seeded and closed: **12 items**, ДБН А.3.1-5:2016 Додаток Н,
positions **Н.14** (внутрішні санітарно-технічні роботи) and **Н.15** (монтаж
електротехнічних установок). A CHECK at `0041_requirement_rules_bound_to_the_baseline.sql:198`
makes any other position **unrepresentable**. There is no create/update/delete
operation for library content in either scope CSV. **On any other kind of work
there are no requirements at all**, which binds every pilot regardless of
screens.

The standard itself says the binding list for a site comes from **робоча
документація** (п. 8.4.3.3) and that Додаток Н is **довідковий** — allow-list
item 8 of
[`docs/product/hidden-works-content-rules.md`](docs/product/hidden-works-content-rules.md).
So the seeded set is a starter by design, not a half-filled reference.

**The owner's decision: add a verification category for project-supplied
sources** and let a workspace author requirements from its own working
documentation. What that entails, none of it started:

- A change to an **Approved** document under change control.
  `hidden-works-content-rules.md` «restricts at every precedence level,
  **including over ADRs**», and its verification vocabulary has exactly three
  values — `VERIFIED_PRIMARY`, `VERIFIED_SECONDARY`, `UNVERIFIED`, the last of
  which «must never be shown as normative». A requirement from a site's working
  documentation is none of those.
- INV-073 requires the text to travel with its tag **and** its source or not at
  all. «Робоча документація» without a sheet and a drawing number is a word, not
  a source. Deciding what a ПТВ types there is the product work.
- Then the mechanics: a route, a capability, and a decision whether project-
  sourced items extend `requirement_library_items` or live in their own table so
  the seeded verified set cannot be corrupted.

**One stale record to close while you are in there:** that document's Open items
say the primary ДБН file's «retrieval URL, retrieval date and hash» were never
recorded. They are recorded — in the CSV rows themselves:
`https://e-construction.gov.ua/laws_detail/3879707932224390963`, завантажено
2026-08-10, `sha256=4592eda…`, matching the header's own «CLOSED 2026-08-10».
The document contradicts itself, and this is the item that closes one of M0's
twelve exit gates.

### 5.2 Plan D slice D3 — the contract chain

Decided scope: **every step as its own screen, including amendments.** Not
started; no branch. Research established the following, and none of it should be
re-derived:

- **The dashboard has no write plumbing at all.** `apps/app/src/lib/api.ts`
  exports one function, `apiGet`. There is no server action anywhere. Every one
  of D3's nine steps is a command needing an `Idempotency-Key` **header**.
  D0–D2 were pure reads.
- **Half the chain has no read operation.** There is no `contracts.list`, no
  `contract_versions.list`, no list of rule versions and none of bindings.
  Version discovery means probing `versionNo` 1, 2, 3… until 404, and getting a
  version's uuid requires a GET by number first. «Every step as its own screen»
  needs new query operations, which ADR-009 restricts and for which D1 already
  spent one dated exception.
- **An amendment is a full retype.** `POST /v1/contracts/{id}/versions` writes
  one row; nothing copies lines forward, rule bindings start empty, and binding
  is append-only with no unbind. Publishing v2 does **nothing** to existing
  assignments or occurrences.
- **Ordering trap.** A work item carrying `workTypeKey` is refused unless a rule
  version with that key is already **published** in the workspace
  (`app.work_type_key_is_bindable`). Rules are published **before** classified
  lines. An untyped line is legal and produces zero occurrences — the empty
  obligation screen the owner hit on a phone on 2026-08-21.
- **Assignment creation returns `coverage`** ∈ `covered | no_bindings |
  no_matching_rule | work_type_unresolved`. It is the only place the reason for
  an empty obligation set reaches a user. Render it.
- **`members.list` returns `{memberId, userId, role, status}`** — no email, no
  name. An assignee picker would show UUIDs. This is D4's problem and it lands
  in D3's form.
- `PUT /v1/parties/{id}/legal-profile` is **PUT**, an upsert: 201 then 200.
  `own_legal_profiles.manage` is **owner only**, not admin.
- Publish needs a client-computed `confirmedManifestHash` and refuses 409
  `RULE_BINDING_REQUIRED` with nothing bound, 422 with no lines. `contracts.edit`
  and `rule_bindings.manage` are **not** implied by `project.admin`.
- **The fixture that already drives the whole chain** is
  `apps/app/tests/field-capture.int.test.ts`'s `boundOccurrence()`. Reuse it
  rather than reinventing the order.

## 7. How this session worked, and the one failure mode to expect

Implementation ran subagent-driven: one implementer per task, a task review, fix
rounds capped at five, then a whole-branch review with independent lenses whose
non-Minor findings each went to a verifier told to **refute by default**. That
verification is not ceremony — it refuted findings that would have cost real
rounds, and twice it caught the controller (me) being wrong.

**Run the gate yourself.** Every number in this document was re-run by the
controller on the tree being offered, not carried from a report. One report
claimed 528 failing tests that did not exist — it was an unset `APP_DB_URL`, and
the cause had been asserted rather than read.

**Expect this failure mode: confidently wrong comments.** Across four slices,
review caught more than a dozen comments that argued a mechanism, were wrong,
and reached the right conclusion anyway — one so wrong that Tailwind compiled
the very classes out of the comment denying they could compile. They pass every
gate here because comments compile. The rules that stuck:

- Do not state a mechanism you have not executed or read at a specific
  `file:line`. «I could not establish this, and here is what I checked» is the
  acceptable form.
- **No counts in source comments.** A count is measured once and wrong
  thereafter. `TODOS.md`'s migration entry is the one home for them, with the
  date and the command.
- **No claims about what exists in other files.** Four such claims were made in
  one round and all four were false.
- Cite the **symbol**, not the line. A line number rots on the next edit — one
  citation was wrong three times in a row, twice invalidated by the very commit
  correcting it.
- When a sentence keeps producing wrong versions of itself, **delete it**.

**And run the browser.** Three defects this week were invisible to diff review
and to reading: a `data-[…]` variant beating `touch:` on specificity (36px where
44 was required), a Radix primitive silently dropping `className`, and a
hairline under a table's last row on a public page. Slice D0's harness went six
consecutive green runs straight through a real bug because its check returned by
a route that wiped the state the bug lived in. **A green browser pass is
evidence about the paths it walks and nothing else** — which is why every new
route gets an audit, and why the two routes that got one each surfaced a real
defect on their first run.
