## Evidence source

The 2026-07-28 lead workbook is the only outreach evidence base referenced by
this log (decision D-042). Recipient email addresses are deliberately never
copied into documentation; the workbook remains the private source for
addresses.

## What is reported but not evidenced

The founder reports outreach to all 50 companies. Send evidence for the 28
legacy-source records and the one unsent queue record is not present in the
workbook, so those additional sends remain **unreconciled** founder-reported
activity. Until reconciled, documentation must distinguish:

1. 50 companies in the lead map;
2. 21 sends evidenced by the workbook on 2026-07-28;
3. additional sends reported by the founder but not proven by the workbook.

## What outreach counts do and do not mean

Lead count and send count are activity metrics, not demand validation.
Validation evidence — replies, artifact access, interviews, named projects,
pilot commitments, willingness to pay — is tracked separately in
[validated-assumptions.md](validated-assumptions.md).

## Log maintenance rule

New outreach appends dated entries here with the evidence source named (the
workbook version or another artifact). A reply, meeting, or pilot commitment
is recorded in both this log (as an event) and the validated-assumptions
ledger (as evidence against a named assumption). No entry may contain a
recipient email address.
