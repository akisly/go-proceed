## What the numbers actually say

The project reads its own position as «21 sends, zero replies». That is a fair
count and a misleading conclusion.

`outreach-log.md` evidences **21 sends on one day — 2026-07-28** — and records
no follow-up since. As of today that is **26 days with one touch**. Zero replies
to a single cold email is the expected outcome of a single cold email; it is
not information about the market, and treating it as such is the mirror image of
the error `validated-assumptions.md`'s own validation rule forbids — that rule
stops activity being read as validation, and this reads activity as
*invalidation*.

**What is genuinely known:** eight assumptions unvalidated, zero signals of any
kind on record, and one founder-reported conversation (2026-08-05) with several
companies who confirmed the problem and photograph work through Telegram — with
no names, no dates, no notes, no documents.

**What the ledger itself names as the sharpest risk**, and it does not depend on
any of that: `A-6`. Contract-baseline import was specified against **zero real
files**. The project holds no акт, no кошторис, no КБ-2в, no виконавча
документація. The ledger's stated minimum to move it is one sentence long:
«one real sanitized кошторис or АВР file».

## What changed yesterday, and why it matters here

Until 2026-08-22 the product had nothing that could be shown to an outsider
without asking them for something first — an account, an install, a call.

That is no longer true. **A технагляд can now open a link with no account and
see the photograph**, beside the requirement in the standard's own wording with
its ДБН source, and accept or return it. No registration, no app, no password.

This is the only part of GoProceed that costs the recipient nothing to look at,
and it is the part the demand scan says adoption most plausibly runs through
(«a no-account review link, neutral receipt, visible sources»). It should be the
opening move of every contact from here, not a feature listed later in a deck.

## Three asks, ordered by how cheap they are to say yes to

The research is explicit that a pilot is justified only once **one company
provides a real artifact and names a project** where a hidden-work cycle will
occur within 30 days. That is the destination. These are the steps that reach it
without asking for it up front.

### Ask 1 — «подивіться» (costs the recipient ~40 seconds)

Send the no-account review link. They click, see a real photo against a real
requirement, and press accept or return. Nothing is asked of them.

**Moves:** `A-3` (external reviewers will decide inside a protected link without
an account) — this is the one assumption a single click can produce evidence
for, because opening the link IS artifact access, the ledger's second-strongest
evidence kind. It is logged automatically.

### Ask 2 — «один файл» (costs them one attachment)

One кошторис or АВР, sanitized however they like — prices blacked out, names
replaced. Explicitly: we do not need a whole project, a current file, or a
correct one.

**Moves:** `A-6`, and it is the only thing that can. It is also the smallest
possible ask that produces a customer document, which the project has never had.

### Ask 3 — «двадцять хвилин» (costs them time — ask last, not first)

The interview below. Artifact-first, not opinion-first, per the scan's own
instruction.

**Moves:** `A-1`, `A-8`, and possibly `A-2`.

## Material 3 — interview guide, 20 minutes, artifact-first

The scan's instruction is to request artifacts rather than opinions. Every
question below asks for a number, a document, or a count of events that already
happened. **None asks whether they would use anything.**

**Before (2 min).** Which role are you — ПТВ, виконроб, керівник? How many
active objects right now?

**The last five returns (6 min) — the core of the interview.**
1. Take the last five closings that came back. What was the stated reason for
   each? (Ask for the exact wording, not the category.)
2. How many of the five came back because of documents rather than work?
3. How long did each take from the return to the re-submission?

**One act, reconstructed (5 min).**
4. Take one акт на приховані роботи from the last month. Where were its photos
   before they were in it?
5. Who assembled it, and how long did that take?
6. Was the photo taken **before** covering, or reconstructed afterwards?
7. How many phone calls did assembling it require, and to whom?

**Money and time (4 min).**
8. From work completed to payment included — what is the usual span, and what
   is the worst you have had this year?
9. What is currently waiting on documents rather than on work?

**Technical supervision (3 min).**
10. Who signs on the customer's side, and would that person open a link from
    you if it required no account?
11. Has a supervisor ever refused because the relationship to the general
    contractor was unclear?

**Close.** Who decides whether a team uses a tool like this — and who pays?
(One question, asked last, because asking it first turns the conversation into
a sale.)

## Material 4 — the demo, when there is a call

Show one thing, in this order, and stop.

1. **Their side first.** The link. The photo, the requirement with its source,
   accept or return. Say plainly: no account, and it works on a phone.
2. **The foreman's side.** One tap to capture, and the receipt showing the
   server's own hash. Say plainly: if this is slower than sending a photo to a
   group chat, it will not be used — the scan says so and we believe it.
3. **The office side, last.** Photos found by доручення rather than by
   scrolling, with the time the server received them, how they were captured,
   and the hash.

**Do not show or promise:** contract-baseline import against their file. It has
never met a real file, and that is precisely what Ask 2 exists to fix. Saying
otherwise is the fastest way to lose the one thing this stage needs.

## How this gets recorded

`outreach-log.md`'s maintenance rule requires a dated entry per outreach event,
naming its evidence source, and never containing an address. Each of the three
asks produces a different evidence kind, and they enter the ledger at different
strengths — a click is artifact access, a reply is a reply, a file is neither
until it comes with a named company and a date.

**Nothing in this document moves an assumption on its own.** Under the ledger's
rule an assumption moves only on direct evidence from a **named** external
person or organization, and it is recorded with a dated artifact.
