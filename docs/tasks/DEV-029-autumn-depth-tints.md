# DEV-029 — Autumn: ground rhythm, warm tints, light and glass on the landing

## Assignment

- Objective and user-visible outcome: the landing keeps every page, block and composition it has, and stops reading as ink on paper at seven opacities. **Rewritten 2026-09-22 after the owner rejected two revisions and an `impeccable` critique measured the cause.** The outcome is a GRAMMAR, not a vocabulary: four legal forms of block (paper, a white sheet with `shadow-float`, one warm stage, a full-bleed band), ground bound to the OBJECT rather than to the section, two vertical rhythms instead of six, the four decorative tints confined to the roles grid, and no dark ground anywhere.
