# DEV-027 — The landing's pointer-reactive grounds, hovers and particle sphere, after its reference

## Assignment

- Objective and user-visible outcome: the four landing pages of DEV-025/DEV-026 take the reference's (https://parlo-black.vercel.app/) *behaviour* — the animations, hovers and pointer reactions DEV-026 missed because it worked from still screenshots. The hero's pixel field is deeper, carries a light centre and lights up under the pointer with a smooth decay; the fact band's grid does the same; every card and row answers a hover; the requirement sources sit in the reference's logo-row composition; a particle sphere turns under the fact tiles; the closing block's ground is a field of arcs that bends to the pointer. Colours stay Daylight's, the four-page split stays ours.

## Reference observations

Observed live on 2026-09-19 with puppeteer 25.8.0 at 1440 × 900 (software WebGL: `--use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader --ignore-gpu-blocklist --enable-webgl`; with `--disable-gpu` the reference renders its own error boundary). Method: a DOM dump (every `<canvas>` with its size, position, context type and `pointer-events`; `document.getAnimations()`; computed transitions; `:hover` rules), contact sheets of frames at rest, frames while the pointer crosses a block and frames after it stops and after it leaves, full-resolution crops round the pointer, per-frame pixel differences against the frame at rest, and computed styles before and after a hover. The owner's saved HTML snapshot was read for structure and hover classes. Nothing was copied — no code, CSS, shader or asset — and no screenshot of the reference is in the repository; the captures are in the session scratchpad.

**Inventory.** 16 `<canvas>`: two 2D in the hero (664 × 378 each, left and right halves, `pointer-events: none`); four 2D inside the 01–04 columns; eight 2D, one in each 50px dotted band; one 2D across the closing block (1378 × 568, `pointer-events: none`); one WebGL 2 (three.js r185) under the statistics, 2158 × 2158, of which only the top cap is inside the section. CSS loops: `star-btn` 3 s on every primary pill, `testimonial-scroll` 48 s, `cta-model-marquee` 36 s, `cta-center-breathe` 4 s.
