# 14. Go-to-market and pilot playbook

## 1. Beachhead

Ukraine-based electrical subcontractors with 15–100 staff, 2–10 concurrent commercial/industrial sites, monthly progress/AVR process and recurring evidence/acceptance friction. Buyer: owner/commercial director. Champion: PTO head/project manager. Do not start with general contractors, tiny crews, state mega-project procurement or residential B2C.

## 2. Trigger events

- package/act returned or cut;
- rapid growth to several sites;
- new GC/customer with strict proof;
- hidden works created dispute;
- owner cannot reconcile performed vs billable;
- PTO spends close week collecting chats/photos;
- customer requests digital/structured format.

## 3. Discovery channel

Build a named account list from open company registers, construction association/member directories, tender/award data, job listings for electrical/MEP project roles, project signage, supplier/estimator referrals, LinkedIn and existing GC ecosystems. Verify active sites and specialization manually. Contact with a concrete hypothesis, not mass spam.

Example message:

> Допомагаю електромонтажним підрядникам бачити ще до закриття періоду, які виконані роботи не пройдуть подання через відсутні фото, обсяги або погодження. Досліджую останній реальний пакет, який повернули/урізали. Чи готові показати знеособлений приклад на 30 хвилин? Нічого продавати на першій зустрічі не буду.

## 4. Sales process

1. Qualification: ICP, trigger, value at risk, sponsor, live project, procurement.
2. Artifact discovery: last estimate/package/evidence/rejection.
3. Workflow map and quantified baseline.
4. Tailored prototype/readiness audit.
5. Pilot order: scope, 45 days, one site, users, data, outcomes, weekly ritual.
6. Technical/privacy/security check.
7. Kickoff and first value in 7 days.
8. Midpoint evidence/readiness review.
9. Period close outcome review and paid conversion proposal.

## 5. Pilot success contract

Required:

- paid pilot 15,000–30,000 UAH per site (doc 10; 0 only under signed design-partner exception) — pilot is a conversion/CAC investment, not a margin unit;
- named executive sponsor, PTO lead, foreman and billing user;
- one live project with ≥100 meaningful work lines or agreed subset;
- baseline last close: hours, returned value, days, evidence misses;
- weekly 30-minute operating review;
- permission to use anonymized aggregate result only with written approval.

Success targets: ≥80% relevant field captures through AktFlow, ≥60% performed value evidence-ready before close, zero unrecoverable before-concealment misses in scoped work, one package reconciled, weekly office/field participation. Commercial result is contextual, not guaranteed.

Измеримость targets: `≥60% evidence-ready`, `one package reconciled`, `weekly participation` считаются напрямую из событий (doc 11 §1/§4). Три target-а измеряются НЕ деревом событий, а ручным baseline/аудитом — знаменатель включает работу мимо продукта: `≥80% relevant field captures` (знаменатель = все релевантные фиксации, включая сделанные в мессенджерах — ручной аудит когорты), `closing prep time −30%` (сравнение с пред-пилотным ручным замером, doc 01 §7 / §5 baseline), `zero unrecoverable misses` (истинные miss-ы могли не попасть в систему). Эти три помечаются в отчёте пилота как «manual baseline», чтобы не создавать иллюзии автоматической телеметрии.

Checkpoint-сетка 30/60/90 (поверх 45-дневного default-пилота): **день 30** — midpoint evidence/readiness review: ≥80% captures, ≥50% performed value evidence-ready, 0 unrecoverable misses; **день 45–60** — закрытие цикла: один reconciled package, ≥60% evidence-ready, решение о renewal/оплате; **день 90** — retention-контроль: второй период закрыт самостоятельно (без concierge-вмешательства), рефлексия по baseline-метрикам последнего «ручного» закрытия.

## 6. Onboarding playbook

Day 0 data checklist; day 1 mapping workshop; day 2 rule workshop; day 3 field training on site; days 4–7 first capture/review; week 2 readiness ritual; week 3 blocker/close preparation; close week package and manual submission receipt; result review.

Opening balance: объёмы, выполненные до старта пилота, НЕ вносятся как полевые фиксации и не участвуют в evidence-ready метриках. Пилот доказывает только текущий период; довнедренческий прогресс виден как разница contracted − available в импортированной baseline (agreed subset строк) и явно помечается в дашборде как «до AktFlow, без доказательной цепочки». Попытка «дозаписать» историю задним числом ловится authoritative reporting-date контролем (doc 38). Variation pain is recorded for GA discovery but the gated variation runtime is not silently enabled in Pilot. Founder maintains issue log separating product bug, data issue, rule/config, behavior and external dependency.

## 7. Objection handling

- **“We have Viber/Telegram.”** Show missing link from line/quantity to complete proof and period readiness.
- **“GC forces its system.”** AktFlow is subcontractor-owned master record and exports/pushes the required package.
- **“We use Excel/AVK.”** Keep estimate/accounting; demonstrate field proof and review gap.
- **“Foremen won’t fill forms.”** Test a 30–60 second task offline (hard ceiling 90 seconds per kill criteria); remove nonessential input.
- **“No budget.”** Compare subscription/pilot to last measurable delayed/unsupported amount and PTO labor.
- **“Market/regulation may change.”** Core is evidence/version/readiness; formats are adapters.

## 8. Marketing assets

Landing, 3-minute role demo, one-page “readiness before AVR”, sample redacted package, security one-pager, pilot brief, ROI worksheet, implementation checklist and later verified case study. Never publish invented customer logos, metrics or regulatory guarantees.

## 9. Channel expansion

After direct founder sales validates message: estimating consultants, MEP suppliers, construction accountants, industry associations and implementation partners. Referral payout must not compromise advice. Partners receive a defined implementation kit and certification only after repeatability.

## 10. GTM kill/iterate signals

- interviews confirm pain but no buyer owns budget → reposition buyer/workflow;
- field adoption <40% despite task redesign → shift to supervisor capture or integration;
- GC policy forbids parallel record and export cannot satisfy it → target different segment/system connector;
- only bespoke document work closes → productize one template pack or exit services trap;
- no paid conversion after three well-run pilots → pause build and reassess wedge/pricing.
