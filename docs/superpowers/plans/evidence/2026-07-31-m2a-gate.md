## Operations steps NOT performed

Recorded as outstanding rather than claimed as done:

- **The evidence purge worker is not scheduled.** `0021` schedules only the
  expiry marking, which is pure SQL. Deleting bytes needs storage credentials,
  so the worker (`apps/app/src/lib/evidence-purge.ts`) has to be wired to a
  runtime that holds them. Until then the 24-hour guarantee in INV-047 is
  demonstrated by tests, not operating in an environment.
- **`pg_cron` scheduling is unverified outside local.** Both `0021` and M1's
  `0005` guard on the extension's presence and log a notice when it is absent.
  Whether the job actually exists on staging is a staging check.
- **No CI run.** CI triggers on push to main or on a pull request; this branch
  has neither.

## What the slice delivers

Eleven operations: the seven catalogued `v0.1-M2` operations plus
`requirement_templates.create`/`.publish` pulled forward from M3, and — counting
honestly — `assignments.list` and `upload_intents.get` are the two queries in
that seven.

Seven migrations. `0015` module DDL, `0016` security, then four corrections from
the engineering review (`0017` definer authorization, `0018` capture-event
provenance, `0019` INV-023 scope plus physical key uniqueness plus the upload
write surface, `0020` the evidence bucket) and `0021` purge.

Vertical scenario (`apps/app/tests/vertical-m2a.int.test.ts`), nine ordered
steps: publish a template, pin an assignment to that exact version, record
progress and check the pool reconciles, authorize and stage and finalize an
upload, prove the evidence identity immutable, replay finalize for an identical
receipt, correct the quantity down and watch money return with no root in
deficit, revoke authorization mid-flight and get `orphaned_for_purge` with no
evidence row, then purge the orphaned bytes and confirm the evidence survives.

## Review findings resolved on this branch

All ten open items from the `## GSTACK REVIEW REPORT` in the plan are closed.
The four that changed the design:

- **D1** — valuation reconciled in aggregate while corrupting per-root lineage,
  and the property test defending it was a telescoping tautology. Replaced with
  per-root allocation: positive quantity carves from the unperformed pool,
  negative re-proportions within its own root.
- **D3** — bucket-to-bucket promotion cannot be atomic with the database
  transaction. The move was deleted rather than journalled; one bucket, one
  immutable key issued at authorization.
- **D4** — `drain_outbox` marks every claimed row processed with no dispatch, so
  purge got its own queue and worker rather than riding the outbox.
- **D7** — inspection recorded `passed` after re-reading the client's own MIME
  string. It now sniffs magic bytes and requires them to agree.

## Defects found in v0.1-M1 while building this slice

- **A cross-tenant hole in this slice's own first draft.** Both `SECURITY
  DEFINER` allocation-head functions took the workspace from the caller. A
  member of one workspace planted a balance row in another; fixed in `0017` with
  a test that reproduces the attack.
- **Zero-priced rows broke publish with a 500.** `unit_price_decimal` was
  derived from whether a price parsed, and `0,00` parses truthy. Any estimate
  containing work bundled into another line failed to publish. Fixed with a
  regression test; recorded in `TODOS.md`.
- **The capability vocabulary lives in three places** and only two were
  extended, so every M2 grant was rejected with a 422 that the fixtures
  swallowed. `packages/testing/src/capability-vocabulary.test.ts` now guards it.
- **`import_batches.publish` shipped on the 30-day idempotency class.** Corrected
  to `ledger_400d` with a retention test.

## Testing posture

Every load-bearing guarantee was mutation-checked: the assertion was shown to
fail when the mechanism it claims to test is removed. That applies to the
valuation reconciliation, the work-item lock, the INV-025 reserve guard, the
INV-047 recheck, the template version race, the contract-version pin, bucket
privacy, and the no-stored-signed-URL rule.

Money fixtures span the tax mode × price state × valuation basis matrix, reached
through the real import path rather than by seeding rows, because `work_items`
are immutable and a seeded fixture would not prove the importer produces those
shapes. That choice is what surfaced the zero-price defect.
